07 · Motif, imagery & icons

Abstract originals, used sparingly

Rhythm pattern

Dots and arcs, 40px tile. Once per screen at most, behind sparse content.

Arc motif

The mark’s arcs, cropped large in a corner at 50% opacity. One per composition.

Waveform

Audio is first-class. Loading audio pulses slowly — no skeleton shimmer.

Warm radial

Clay and marigold in opposite corners. Hero moments only, once per flow.

Imagery

Golden-hour, warm-biased photography of specific, named and credited people. Text over photos needs the ink gradient overlay (45% → 70%). Crop portraits at the eyes’ third; circles for people, rounded rectangles for places and objects. Until real photography exists, use the tonal placeholders — never stock clichés.

Icons

Lucide, 2px stroke, rounded caps. 20px default, 24px in tab bars (inactive in subtle text colour, active in brand clay). Never emoji or unicode shapes as icons. Lucide is a placeholder until an Igbo-context set is commissioned.

Cultural IP

Uli, nsibidi and akwete belong to specific communities and artists. They are never used as decoration here. Any traditional pattern must be sourced from an Igbo artist, with attribution and compensation.

Motion

  • Unhurried: 220ms ease-out by default. Entries fade and rise 8px.
  • Section transitions cross-fade; sheets slide with ease-in-out.

Data visualisation series

  • Single series: clay. Comparison: clay vs. marigold. Use series in this order; never more than six.
  • Label values directly; avoid legends when you can. Gridlines in hairline colour or none.
  • Always cite the source under the chart. Sample data must say so.
